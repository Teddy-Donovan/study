DSP mock A

Instructions. Time allowed: 2 hours. Open book: hard copy material is permitted. Attempt all questions. There are 60 marks in total. Marks for each part are given in brackets. Show your working: method marks are given even when a number is wrong.

Budget about 2 minutes per mark. Leave 5 minutes at the end to check units, ROCs and signs. If you get stuck on an early part, use the “assuming the answer to part (…) is …” value and keep going.

This is a mock sat 4 days before the DSP exam. It covers L02–L10: about 25% on L02–L04 (Q1–Q2, 15 marks) and 75% on L05–L10 (Q3–Q8, 45 marks). Notation follows the course: and for continuous time, and for discrete time, and .

Score interpretation

Add up your marks out of 60. Then look at where you lost them, because that matters more than the total.

Score What it means What to do in the next 3 days
51–60 (85%+) Exam-ready. Your methods are reliable under time pressure. Do mock B if available. Spend the rest on your reference sheet and on speed.
42–50 (70–84%) Solid. A few method gaps or slips. Reread the packs for the two questions where you lost the most marks. Redo those questions from a blank page without the solution.
30–41 (50–69%) Pass level, but fragile. Probably one or two whole topics are weak. Spend one study block on each weak topic (pack sections 2 and 3, then the new practice questions). Put the method steps on your reference sheet.
Below 30 Big gaps. Most likely in L05–L10, which carry 45 of the 60 marks. Go in this order: D8 (inverse z), D11 (Butterworth), D4 (frequency response), D5 (DFT). These are mechanical and give the most marks per hour.
